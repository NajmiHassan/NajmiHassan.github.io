I'm Najmi Hassan, a Software Engineering graduate (BS, Minhaj University Lahore) working at the intersection of **machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision**. I'm especially drawn to **multi-agent systems**, designing AI that can reason, coordinate, and carry out complex tasks autonomously. I'm also increasingly interested in cybersecurity and robotics, and I'm open to research and collaboration in these areas. I work mostly in Python with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn, and I enjoy taking ideas straight from research papers to working, user-facing tools.

Teaching is a big part of who I am. I've led **Generative AI** workshops for 500+ participants, served as a **Stanford Code in Place section leader** teaching CS106A, and mentored underprivileged students in programming and data analysis. I care about making technical knowledge accessible and helping others start their own journey in AI.
