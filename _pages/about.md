I'm Najmi Hassan, a Software Engineering graduate (BS, Minhaj University Lahore) working at the intersection of machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision. I'm especially drawn to multi-agent systems — designing AI that can reason, coordinate, and carry out complex tasks autonomously — and I'm currently exploring cybersecurity and robotics as new directions for my work.

I like to learn by building. My projects range from a CNN-based image forgery detection system and a DCGAN that synthesizes anime faces from scratch, to a five-agent pipeline that turns any research topic into a fully cited report, plus interpretable ML models for domains like mental-health screening and air-quality prediction. I work mostly in Python with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn, and I enjoy taking ideas straight from research papers to working, user-facing tools.

Teaching is a big part of who I am. I've led Generative AI workshops for 500+ participants, served as a Stanford Code in Place section leader teaching CS106A, and mentored underprivileged students in programming and data analysis. I care about making technical knowledge accessible and helping others start their own journey in AI.
