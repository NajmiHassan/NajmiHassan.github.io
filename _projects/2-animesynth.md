**Tools and Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, Kaggle API, Google Colab (GPU)

- Implemented a Deep Convolutional GAN from scratch based on the DCGAN research paper (Radford et al., 2015) to synthesize realistic anime faces from random noise
- Generated 64x64 anime face images with progressive quality improvement through training loops
