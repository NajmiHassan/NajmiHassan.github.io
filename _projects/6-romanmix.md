**Tools and Technologies:** Python, Scikit-learn, Gradio, Logistic Regression

- Built a token-level language identification system for Roman Urdu–English mixed text
- Engineered a full NLP pipeline covering manual seed corpus creation, DictVectorizer-based feature extraction, and hard-rule overrides for URLs, punctuation, and emoji
- Achieved 0.78 macro-average F1 across all five classes under 5-fold cross-validation, with 0.95 overall accuracy masking class imbalance across rare categories
